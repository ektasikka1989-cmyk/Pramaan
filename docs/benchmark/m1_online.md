### Q2 What JSY incentive does an ASHA get on completing a pregnant woman's ANC check-ups in a rural area?

For a rural area, the supplied evidence states that ASHAs are entitled to a composite and uniform incentive under JSY of Rs. 600 per delivery in rural areas, and Rs. 400 per delivery in urban areas, throughout the country [2].

The evidence does not separately state the ANC-only portion for a rural area.

For reference, for an urban area the evidence breaks the Rs. 400 total into Rs. 200 for motivating women for completing all ante-natal check ups and Rs. 200 for facilitating her delivery in a health institution [1]. No equivalent rural ANC vs delivery split is given in the supplied evidence, so the rural ANC-only amount cannot be established from it.

### Q3 What is the zinc dose for a 4-month-old baby with diarrhoea, and for how many days?

For a 4-month-old baby with diarrhoea, the dose is half tablet - that is 10 mg of zinc [1] [2].

This is because one zinc tablet in the blister pack contains 20 mg of zinc, and for children 2 to 6 months age, half tablet is to be given [1] [2].

It is to be given for 14 days, as zinc is only given to children 2 months up to 5 years for 14 days [1].

For giving it at home, take a clean teaspoon, request the mother to express milk from her breast into the spoon and then add the half tablet, and discard the remaining half tablet [2].

### Q4 How should a mother prepare ORS at home for a child with diarrhoea?

To prepare ORS at home for a child with diarrhoea:

**1. Using an ORS packet**

1. Wash your hands thoroughly with soap and water [2].
2. Pour all the ORS powder from a packet into a clean container [2].
3. Measure one litre of clean drinking water and pour it into the container in which you poured ORS [1] [2].
 - If you have ORS packets for 1/2 litre of water then take 1/2 litre water [1] [2].
4. Stir until all the powder in the container has been mixed with water and none remain at the bottom of the container [1] [2].
5. Taste ORS solution before giving it to the child. It should taste like tears - neither too sweet nor too salty [1] [3].
 - If it tastes too sweet or too salty then throw away the solution and prepare ORS solution again [1] [3].
6. Any ORS solution which is left over after 24 hours should be thrown away [1] [3].

Show the caregiver how to prepare ORS and explain how much ORS is to be given in addition to the usual fluid intake [1].

### Q5 What should be applied to the umbilical cord stump of a newborn?

Nothing should be applied to the umbilical cord stump.

It is to be kept clean and dry [1], left uncovered and not bound or bandaged. A normal stump is clean, dry, and tied properly [1].

It slowly dries up and falls off by 7 to 10 days [1].

### Q6 What should the ASHA check about breastfeeding during a home visit?

During a home visit, the ASHA should check breastfeeding as part of assessing the mother and child:

* Use the home-visit checklist, ask key questions, observe the mother and child, monitor weight and look for danger signs or other health problems [1].
* Teach correct breastfeeding techniques, support exclusive breastfeeding, help with breastfeeding problems and explain responsive feeding [1].
* For young children, counsel on complementary feeding from six months, dietary diversity and continued breastfeeding up to two years and beyond [1].
* Counsel the family about continued breastfeeding during illness [2].
* Provide correct and complete information to families, clear misconceptions and myths, and include basic breast conditions and their management [4].

The visit app helps by showing the right counselling points for that family based on what is entered, including guidance on breastfeeding, so counselling is accurate and the same for every family, with short videos to show the mother and family [3].

The evidence does not list a separate detailed breastfeeding observation checklist beyond these points.

### Q7 What are the danger signs in a newborn that need immediate referral?

In a newborn or infant, the following signs indicate an emergency and need urgent medical attention with immediate referral:

1. Baby is unusually sleepy, lethargic, or unconscious and limp [1]
2. Baby is not accepting feeds or vomiting everything [1]
3. Baby is having fits [1]
4. Baby has fever or is hypothermic - temperature is below normal [1]
5. Baby has fast breathing > 60 breaths per minute [1]

In addition to these general danger signs, there may be other signs like fast breathing, chest indrawing, stiff neck, etc. in other diseases that require immediate referral [2].

What to do:
Presence of danger signs needs immediate referral to the nearby facility using free referral transport like 102/108 National Ambulance Service [1]. Supportive care like feeding and hydration is essential in transit and during illness [1].

Note: The source list continues beyond point 5 with "6...." but the full detail for further signs was not included in the supplied evidence, so those additional signs cannot be stated here.

### Q8 A 7-day-old baby has fever and is feeding less. What should the ASHA do, and what before reaching the hospital?

A 7-day-old baby with **fever + feeding less** has danger signs.

What ASHA should do:

* **Assess the baby promptly** - including body temperature - and watch for other danger signs like poor feeding, difficulty breathing, less movement, fever, cold to touch [2].
* **Refer promptly** in case of fever or poor feeding or inability to feed [2].
* **Get help immediately** if any of these signs appear [2].

For this combination, the source supports:
* **For fever, provide fever management** [3].
* **For feeding problem, provide breast feeding management** [3].

During examination:
* ASHA should **wash hands with soap and water before touching the baby during each visit** [1].

Limitation: The supplied evidence states that fever management and breastfeeding management should be provided before / along with referral, but it does not include the detailed steps of fever management in the passages provided.

### Q9 Which spacing contraceptive methods are available in the family planning programme?

The spacing contraceptive methods available under the National Family Planning Programme are:

* Injectable contraceptive MPA - under Antara Programme [1] [2]
* Oral Contraceptive Pills including Combined Oral Contraceptive Pills (COCs) (Mala-N / Mala N), non-hormonal Centchroman pills (Chhaya), and Progestin Only Pills (POPs) [1] [2]
* Condoms (Nirodh) [1] [2]
* Intra-Uterine Contraceptive Device - IUCD 380A and IUCD 375 [1] [2]
* Subdermal Contraceptive Implant (Single Rod) [2]

Emergency Contraceptive Pill (Ezy pill) is also listed, but it is not a regular method of contraception [1].

All available methods are provided free of cost through public health facilities and ASHAs [2].

### Q10 Which vaccines are due at birth, and what is the BCG dose?

The vaccines due at birth are BCG, bOPV birth dose, and Hepatitis B (within 24 hours). The BCG dose is 0.05 ml until 1 month of age and 0.1 ml beyond age 1 month. These vaccines are administered intradermally in the upper arm.

### Q11 What is the weekly IFA dose for a school-going adolescent girl under Anaemia Mukt Bharat?

For a school-going adolescent girl in 6th–12th class, the evidence states:

Weekly, one Blue tablet containing 100 mg elemental iron and 0.5 mg folic acid, given round the year [1].

Limitation: The supplied evidence describes this 100 mg + 0.5 mg Blue tablet in the evolution of the Weekly Iron and Folic Acid Supplementation Programme for school-going adolescent boys and girls 6–12th class and out-of-school adolescent girls [1].

A separate passage gives a different Blue WIFS-IFA composition — 60 mg elemental iron + 500 mcg folic acid weekly — but specifically for out-of-school children 10-19 years provided through ASHA during home visits / at AWC [1].

### Q12 A baby weighs 2,200 grams. How often should the ASHA visit?

For high-risk babies, ASHA should:

* If possible, visit the baby every day for a week. Then visit once every three days until the baby is 28 days old, and if the baby is improving, once on the 42nd day [1] [2].
* This is described as increasing the number of home visits after delivery from 5 to 13 [2].
* Advise the mother to keep the baby warm and breastfeed after every two hours [1].
* Weigh the baby during follow-up and fill the home visit form for high-risk baby [1].

Further follow-up from S1:
* If the baby is not gaining weight, and weighs less than 2300 grams on the 28th day, refer the baby to a hospital [1].
* Continue to visit once a week until the baby is two months old, and weigh the baby each time [1].
* Continue to visit the mother and baby until the baby is two years old [1].

This is different from the normal baby schedule of visits on Days 3, 7, 14, 21, 28, 42, then once every two weeks until the baby is two years old [3].

### Q15 दस्त में 4 महीने के बच्चे को जिंक की खुराक क्या है?

4 महीने के बच्चे को दस्त में जिंक की खुराक आधी गोली (10 mg) रोज है [2]।

क्योंकि:
- जिंक केवल 2 महीने से 5 साल तक के बच्चों को 14 दिन तक दिया जाता है [1]
- 2-6 महीने की उम्र में आधी गोली यानी 10 mg दी जाती है [1] 
- एक जिंक गोली में 20 mg जिंक होता है [1] 

घर पर देने का तरीका:
- साफ चम्मच लें 
- माँ से कहें कि चम्मच में अपना दूध निकालें और फिर उसमें आधी गोली डालें 
- बची हुई आधी गोली फेंक दें 
- 2-6 महीने में इसे माँ के दूध या साफ पानी में घोलकर दें [2]

ध्यान रखें: जिंक 14 दिन तक दें, भले ही दस्त रुक जाए [2]

### Q16 घर पर ORS कैसे बनाएं?

घर पर ORS बनाने के 2 तरीके सिखाए गए हैं - ORS पैकेट से और घर पर बना घोल [2]:

**1. ORS पैकेट से घोल बनाना:**

1. अपने हाथ साबुन और पानी से अच्छी तरह धोएं [2]।
2. एक साफ बर्तन में पैकेट का सारा ORS पाउडर डालें [2]।
3. एक लीटर साफ पीने का पानी नाप कर उसी बर्तन में डालें। अगर आपके पास आधा लीटर पानी के लिए ORS पैकेट है तो आधा लीटर पानी लें [1] [2]।
4. तब तक हिलाएं जब तक सारा पाउडर पानी में घुल न जाए और बर्तन के तले में कुछ न बचे [1] [2]।
5. बच्चे को देने से पहले ORS घोल को चखें। इसका स्वाद आंसुओं जैसा होना चाहिए - न ज्यादा मीठा न ज्यादा नमकीन। अगर ज्यादा मीठा या नमकीन लगे तो घोल फेंक दें और फिर से बनाएं [1]।
6. 24 घंटे के बाद बचा हुआ कोई भी ORS घोल फेंक देना चाहिए [1]।

**2. घर पर बना ORS घोल - एक गिलास के लिए:**

हाथ साबुन-पानी से अच्छी तरह धोएं। एक गिलास 200 ml साफ पानी लें, उसमें तीन उंगलियों से एक चुटकी नमक और एक चम्मच चीनी मिलाएं [3]।

### Q18 Baby ko diarrhoea ho to ORS kaise dena chahiye?

To give ORS to a baby with diarrhoea, first clean the child and the caregiver's hands with soap and water. Then, prepare one glass of ORS by mixing 200 ml of clean water, a pinch of salt, and one teaspoon of sugar, stirring until dissolved, and adding half a lime juice. Taste the solution before giving it to the child; if it tastes too sweet or salty, discard it and prepare a new solution. If the child is breastfed, give extra breastfeeds in between ORS.
