## Full answers (for human scoring: completeness, correctness 0–2)

### Q1 How many home visits should an ASHA make for a home-delivered newborn, and on which days?

For a home-delivered newborn, an ASHA should make 5 home visits: on days 3, 7, 14, 21, and 28 after birth.

### Q2 What JSY incentive does an ASHA get on completing a pregnant woman's ANC check-ups in a rural area?

An ASHA gets Rs. 1400 incentive for motivating a pregnant woman to complete all her ante-natal check-ups in a rural area, and an additional Rs. 200 incentive for facilitating her delivery in a health institution.

### Q3 What is the zinc dose for a 4-month-old baby with diarrhoea, and for how many days?

For a 4-month-old baby with diarrhoea, the zinc dose is half a tablet (10 mg) [1]. The zinc supplement should be given for 14 days [1].

### Q4 How should a mother prepare ORS at home for a child with diarrhoea?

To prepare ORS at home for a child with diarrhoea, the mother should first wash her hands thoroughly with soap and water. Then, she should pour all the ORS powder from a packet into a clean container and measure one litre of clean drinking water to add to it. The mixture should be stirred until all the powder is dissolved and none remains at the bottom of the container. Before giving the solution to the child, the mother should taste it to ensure it doesn't taste too sweet or salty, and discard it if necessary. She should also discard any leftover ORS solution after 24 hours.

### Q5 What should be applied to the umbilical cord stump of a newborn?

Nothing should be applied to the umbilical cord stump of a newborn. The cord stump should be kept clean and dry, and it slowly dries up and falls off by 7 to 10 days.

### Q6 What should the ASHA check about breastfeeding during a home visit?

During a home visit, the ASHA should check about the mother's nutrition and breastfeeding techniques. The app guides the ASHA on these points, showing short videos that can be played to demonstrate correct breastfeeding techniques. The ASHA should also assess the health of the mother and child, monitor weight, and look for danger signs or other health problems. Additionally, the ASHA will teach mothers the correct breastfeeding techniques, support exclusive breastfeeding, and help with breastfeeding problems.

### Q7 What are the danger signs in a newborn that need immediate referral?

The danger signs in a newborn that need immediate referral include: the baby is unusually sleepy, lethargic, or unconscious and limp; not accepting feeds or vomiting everything; having fits; having fever or being hypothermic (temperature below normal); fast breathing > 60 breaths per minute; and other signs like chest indrawing, stiff neck, etc. in other diseases that require immediate referral. These signs indicate an emergency and need urgent medical attention. The decision for referral should be jointly made by the ASHA, ANM, CHO, and the family.

### Q8 A 7-day-old baby has fever and is feeding less. What should the ASHA do, and what before reaching the hospital?

The ASHA should ensure the baby's room is warm by using a room heater if necessary (26–28°C) and advise the mother to provide KMC for at least 1-2 hour/session. The ASHA should also monitor the baby's health, including body temperature, and watch for danger signs such as poor feeding, difficulty with breathing, less movement, fever, or cold to touch. If any of these signs appear, the ASHA should get help immediately.

The ASHA should refer the baby to a hospital if the baby has fever and is not improving after providing KMC and ensuring the room is warm.

### Q9 Which spacing contraceptive methods are available in the family planning programme?

The spacing contraceptive methods available in the family planning programme are:
1. Condoms (Nirodh),
2. Combined Oral Contraceptive Pills (COCs) (Mala-N),
3. Centchroman pills (Chhaya),
4. Progestin Only Pills (POPs),
5. Injectable MPA (Antara programme),
6. Intra-Uterine Contraceptive Device (IUCD) 380 A and 375.

### Q10 Which vaccines are due at birth, and what is the BCG dose?

The vaccines due at birth are BCG, bOPV birth dose, and Hepatitis B (within 24 hours). The BCG dose is 0.05 ml until 1 month of age and 0.1 ml beyond age 1 month. These vaccines are administered intradermally in the upper arm.

### Q11 What is the weekly IFA dose for a school-going adolescent girl under Anaemia Mukt Bharat?

School-going adolescent girls under Anaemia Mukt Bharat receive a weekly IFA dose of one Pink WIFS-IFA tablet, which contains 45mg elemental Iron + 400 mcg Folic Acid. The programme aims to improve absorption and prevent anemia by including iron-rich locally available foods and vitamin C along with IFA consumption.

### Q12 A baby weighs 2,200 grams. How often should the ASHA visit?

For a high-risk baby weighing 2,200 grams, the ASHA should visit every day for a week, then once every three days until the baby is 28 days old, and if the baby is improving, once on the 42nd day. The ASHA should also weigh the baby on days 7, 15, 21, and 28. If the baby is not gaining weight or weighs less than 2,200 grams on the 28th day, refer the baby to a hospital.

### Q13 इस मॉड्यूल में कौन-कौन से गैर-संचारी रोग शामिल हैं?

इस मॉड्यूल में उच्च रक्तचाप, मधुमेह तथा तीन सामान्य कैंसरों (बच्चेदानी के मुंह, स्तन तथा मुंह का कैंसर) जैसे गैर-संचारी रोग शामिल हैं।

### Q14 नवजात शिशु में खतरे के लक्षण क्या हैं?

नवजात शिशु में खतरे के लक्षण हैं: सांस लेने में दिक्कत, दूध पीने में असमर्थता, टट्टी या पेशाब करने में असमर्थता, बच्चे की नाल लाल होना, शरीर पर 10 से अधिक छाले, पीलिया, बुखार, दस्त, असक्रिय और सुस्त शरीर, दौरे, आंखें लाल या संक्रमित, और कोई जन्मजात दोष। यदि आपको लगता है कि आपके बच्चे में इनमें से कोई भी लक्षण हैं, तो तुरंत डॉक्टर या एएनएम से संपर्क करें।

### Q15 दस्त में 4 महीने के बच्चे को जिंक की खुराक क्या है?

दस्त में 4 महीने के बच्चे को जिंक की खुराक क्या है?
जिंक खुराक बच्चे के दस्त के इलाज में मदद करती है और उनके विकास और प्रतिरक्षा प्रणाली को समर्थन देती है।
दस्त में 4 महीने के बच्चों को जिंक खुराक की खुराक 10 मिलीग्राम (½ टैबलेट) है, जिसे दूध से मिलाकर पिलाया जाता है।

### Q16 घर पर ORS कैसे बनाएं?

घर पर ORS बनाने के लिए, सबसे पहले एक साफ और स्वच्छ बर्तन लें और उसमें १ लीटर पानी डालें। यदि आपके पास ½ लीटर पानी के लिए ORS पैकेट हैं तो ½ लीटर पानी लें। फिर ORS पाउडर को बर्तन में डालें और अच्छी तरह से मिलाएं। ORS का स्वाद जांचें और यह तय करें कि यह तेल-मीठा या नमकीन नहीं है। यदि यह बहुत मीठा या नमकीन है तो इसे फेंक दें और फिर से ORS बनाएं।

### Q17 गर्भवती महिला की ANC जांच कितनी बार होनी चाहिए?

गर्भवती महिला की ANC जांच को चार बार होना चाहिए, जिसमें प्रत्येक तीन महीने में एक जांच शामिल है। यह जांच प्रारंभिक गर्भावस्था की निगरानी और स्वास्थ्य संबंधी समस्याओं की पहचान करने के लिए महत्वपूर्ण है। प्रत्येक जांच में महिला के वजन, रक्तचाप, रक्त परीक्षण (हीमोग्लोबिन), मूत्र परीक्षण और पेट की जांच शामिल होनी चाहिए।

### Q18 Baby ko diarrhoea ho to ORS kaise dena chahiye?

To give ORS to a baby with diarrhoea, first clean the child and the caregiver's hands with soap and water. Then, prepare one glass of ORS by mixing 200 ml of clean water, a pinch of salt, and one teaspoon of sugar, stirring until dissolved, and adding half a lime juice. Taste the solution before giving it to the child; if it tastes too sweet or salty, discard it and prepare a new solution. If the child is breastfed, give extra breastfeeds in between ORS.
