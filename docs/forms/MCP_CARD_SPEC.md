**Source (official):** *Guidebook for Mother-Child Protection Card* (Revised MCP Card), Ministry of Health and Family Welfare and Ministry of Women and Child Development, Government of India. The guidebook was printed January 2020 and reproduces every page of the revised MCP Card.
**File in repo:** `_claude_work/sources/MCP_Card_Guidebook_MoHFW_MoWCD_2020.pdf`. A copy is also saved as `_claude_work/sources/MCP_Card_2018.pdf`, the name PROMPT_4 expects.

Each section below gives **MCP card page → guidebook PDF page**. Use the field names, row order and options **exactly** as written here. Cite them as: `Source: MCP Card p.N (MoHFW/MoWCD), Guidebook p.M`.

## 2. Regular checkup during pregnancy — MCP card p.4 (guidebook PDF p.14)
Columns are months 1st–9th. Rows:
- Urine Pregnancy Test: Yes/No, Date
- Registration: "Register with the Health Centre in the 1st trimester."
- ANC: "Have at least 3 antenatal checkups, after registration."
- BP, Blood & Urine: "Have blood pressure (BP) checked and blood and urine examined at each visit."
- Weight: "Have weight checkup at each visit. Gain at least 9–11 kg during pregnancy. Gain at least 1 kg every month during the last 6 months of pregnancy."
- T.T. Injection (Td): "Take two T.T. injections. T.T.1 when pregnancy is confirmed and T.T.2 after 1 month. (Fill in the date). Give one dose of T.T. if previously vaccinated within 3 years." The guidebook (PDF p.15) says Td has replaced TT, so label it **Td** with "(TT on older cards)".
- Iron Tablets: "Take one tablet of iron folic acid a day for at least 6 months after first trimester. Take at least 180 tablets. (Fill in quantity and date issued)."
- "Take two tablets of calcium per day for at least 6 months after 1st trimester."
- "Take single dose of tablet albendazole (400 mg) after 1st trimester" + date.

## 4. Danger signs and preparation for delivery — MCP card p.6 (guidebook PDF p.18–19)
Danger signs (take to hospital immediately):
1. Bleeding during pregnancy / excessive bleeding during or after delivery
2. Severe anaemia with or without breathlessness
3. High fever during pregnancy or within one month of delivery
4. Headache, blurring of vision, fits and swelling all over the body
5. Labour pain before term / labour pain for more than 12 hours / reduced foetal movement
6. Bursting of water bag without labour pains / preterm labour pains (< 37 weeks)

Ensure institutional delivery: Contact ASHA/ANM/AWW · Register under JSY, PMMVY (if applicable) · Obtain benefits under JSY · Identify hospital in advance · Arrange for transport in advance · Ensure 48 hours of stay after delivery in the facility.

Home delivery (6 cleans): clean hands, clean surface and surroundings, clean blade, clean thread to tie the cord, clean set of clothes for newborn, clean perineum. Ensure safe delivery by ANM; ensure family care and support.

## 6. Care of Newborn + HBYC — MCP card p.8 (guidebook PDF p.23)
**Please Remember**: Keep the baby warm · Start breastfeeding within 1 hr of birth · Feed the baby only mother's milk · Do not bathe the baby for the first 48 hours · Keep the cord dry · Keep the baby away from sick people · Special care if baby < 2.5 kg at birth.

**Danger Signs — contact your health worker immediately if baby**: Not able to feed · Convulsion · Fast breathing more than 60 breaths per minute · Severe chest indrawing · Axillary temperature 37.5 °C or above (feels hot to touch) · Axillary temperature less than 35.5 °C (feels cold to touch) · Movement only when stimulation or no movement at all.

**Home Based Child Care visits after 6 weeks (✓)** — columns **3 · 6 · 9 · 12 · 15 months**. Cells marked × are not applicable at that age.
- *ASHA to verify at age*: Whether child sick · Breastfeeding continued · Complementary food given: "2–3 tsps of food at a time, 2–3 meals each day with 1–2 snacks between meals" (× at 3m) / "½ cup serving at a time, 2–3 meals each day with 1–2 snacks between meals" (× at 3m) / "¾ to 1 cup serving at a time, 3–4 times a day with 1–2 snacks between meals" (× at 3m) · Weight recording by AWW · Developmental delay checked · Immunization status checked · Measles vaccine given (× at 3m, 6m) · Vitamin A given (× at 3m, 6m, 12m) · ORS at home · IFA syrup at home
- *ASHA to provide services at age*: Counsel for exclusive breastfeeding (× at 9, 12, 15m) · Counsel for complementary feeding (× at 3m) · Counsel for hand washing · Counsel on parenting · Family planning counselling · ORS given · IFA syrup given (× at 3m)

## 8. IFA syrup + deworming compliance card (6 m–5 y) — MCP card p.27 (guidebook PDF p.62)
"Mention date of provision of IFA bottle to mother": Bottle 1–10. Month-wise grid: Jan–Dec × weeks 1–5, across age bands 6–12 month, 1–2, 2–3, 3–4, 4–5 Year. The **family** ticks after each dose.

Albendazole (write date): columns 1–2 Year, 2–3 Year, 3–4 Year, 4–5 Year; rows Dose-1, Dose-2.

Important things to remember:
1. Give IFA syrup every Wednesday and Saturday.
2. Give 1 ml using the auto-dispenser.
3. Don't give it when the child is sick or severely undernourished.
4. Always give it after food.
5. One 50 ml bottle lasts six months.
6. Mark a ✓ after each dose.
7. In case of any problem, contact the ANM immediately.
